# Free home maintenance checklist: monthly, seasonal and repair fund

Rough guidelines. Adjust for your climate, home age and appliances.

## Repair fund

A common rule of thumb is to save 1 to 3 percent of your home's value each year for upkeep. Older homes need the higher end. Put it in a separate savings account so a surprise repair is not a credit card bill.

## Monthly

Test smoke and CO alarms. Check under sinks for leaks. Clean the range hood filter. Run water in unused drains. Check the HVAC filter. Check the fire extinguisher gauge. Look around the water heater for leaks.

## Spring

Clean gutters and downspouts. Inspect the roof from the ground. Service the air conditioner. Check outdoor faucets for freeze damage. Seal driveway cracks. Check the deck and fence. Clean the dryer vent. Test the sump pump.

## Summer

Check window and door seals. Pressure wash siding and deck. Check attic ventilation. Trim trees away from the house. Clean refrigerator coils. Check for pests and wasp nests.

## Fall

Clean gutters again. Service the furnace. Reverse ceiling fans. Drain outdoor hoses and shut off outside faucets. Seal gaps around pipes and doors. Check the chimney. Stock snow tools and salt. Flush the water heater.

## Winter

Check for ice dams. Know where your main water shutoff is. Keep pipes warm in cold snaps. Test flashlights or the generator. Check the basement for moisture.
